Our Story

About Audu Gideon E. and the Academy

Teaching practical digital skills in a simple, hands-on way.

Founder & Lead Trainer

Audu Gideon E.

Audu Gideon E. is a digital practitioner and team lead based in Lagos. For over 10 years he has led teams in operations and hospitality, where he learned that good service comes down to clear systems and patient people. Today he builds websites, AI-powered chatbots and workflow automations for small businesses. He started Eddy-Path Academy to teach these skills in a simple, hands-on way, so that anyone willing to practise can use a computer and digital tools with confidence.
